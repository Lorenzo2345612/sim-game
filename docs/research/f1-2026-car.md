### Deployment taper (MGU-K power vs speed)

Sources conflict on breakpoints. Both are linear to zero.

| Variant | Full 350 kW up to | Zero at | Formula | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regs-quoted (Art. C5.2.8) | 290 km/h | 345 km/h | P_max(kW) = 1800 - 5*v(km/h), clamped to 350 | PRESS quoting article | https://www.formuladream.app/blog/f1-power-unit-explained (via search summary); 290 km/h also at https://www.redbullracing.com/int-en/projects/bulls-guide-to-the-f1-2026-regulations/technical-regulations |
| F1 Briefing base | 280 km/h | 340 km/h | P = 350*(340-v)/60 (derived by the site) | PRESS | https://f1briefing.com/mgu-k-parameters-2026-rules/ |
| Override (following car) | 337 km/h | 355 km/h | P = 350*(355-v)/18 (derived) | PRESS | https://f1briefing.com/mgu-k-parameters-2026-rules/ |

Check: 1800-5v gives 350 at 290 and 0 at 360, not 345; so the 1800/5 formula and the 345 zero point are inconsistent. Recommended model: full to 290, linear to 0 at 345 (slope -6.36 kW per km/h); Override full to 337, zero at 355. Verify C5.2.8 text before finalizing.
