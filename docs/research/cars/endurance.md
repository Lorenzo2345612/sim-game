### Minimal endurance slice ("6 h in 60 min")

1. Race clock = sim time x K (K = 6: 60 min real = 6 h race clock). Lap times stay real (real physics); fuel, tyre wear and ERS harvest are multiplied by K, otherwise laps would not run out of fuel. Example: 90 L tank, 3:25 laps, assumed ~2.4 L/lap real use (author estimate) becomes 14.4 L/lap, so a stint is ~6 laps (~20 min real, 2 h race clock) and 60 min real gives 3 stints. Parameters: `fuelMult`, `tyreWearMult`, `ersHarvestMult` = K; the driver-time ledger counts race-clock hours.
2. Rejected: no compression (60 min = ~17 laps, no endurance feel).
3. Day/night: map race clock to time of day starting at 10:00 or 16:00 and sweep sun with K x real; 6 h covers dusk to full night at K = 6 starting 17:00.
4. Class pace: use section 1 ratios; field of 8 hyper + 8 LMP2 + 12 GT3 cars (existing assets: hyper-a, lmp2-a, gt3-a..d) all on the same track.
5. Mandatory: one stop min, each driver >= 15 % of race clock, FCY and one safety car per race; no weather beyond existing task 139.
6. Real-time option: K = 1 for a 30-60 min "sprint endurance" with 1 pit stop.

Hybrid threshold mapping: `HybridSpec.minSpeedMs` = BoP front-axle threshold (190 km/h = 52.8 m/s); `mgukW` 200 kW LMH front / 50 kW LMDh rear; `deployJPerLap` from the stint energy cap divided by laps per stint.

Proposed `bop` block (new, optional, applied at event load, not edited in car JSON): `powerScale`, `ballastKg`, `energyPerStintMJ`, `hybridMinSpeedKmh`, `fuelFlowScale`. Torque scaling by `powerScale` on the torque curve keeps shape; ballast adds mass at CG.

Peak power from curve: hyper-a 740 Nm at 5000 rpm = 387 kW (engine only, hybrid would reach ~500 kW with 200 kW MGU, matching the 500 kW cap); lmp2-a 600 Nm at 6000 rpm = 377 kW (real ~ 450 kW; consider raising); gt3 690 Nm at 4000 rpm = 289 kW rising to 400 kW at 5500; real GT3 ~ 400-450 kW at the peak, consider raising power (derived, not sourced).
