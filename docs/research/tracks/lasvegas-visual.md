## Overall
- Layout (web): 6.201 km, 17 turns, anticlockwise, ~1.9 km Strip straight; permanent pit/paddock section on the old Harmon/Koval parking land plus public roads on Las Vegas Blvd, Sands Ave, Koval Ln, Harmon Ave. 2023 layout map: img map__.
- Relief: flat desert basin; mountains (brown, ~#8a6f5a) ring the horizon (img aerial, High Roller).
- Environment (img): theme-casino hotels 100-200 m with dense neon, palms and ornamental cypress, wide 6-8 lane Blvd with raised pedestrian bridges, parking structures and low flat commercial blocks between towers; the Sphere (112 m) dominates the far side of the Venetian car park; Fountains and Eiffel proxy at Paris/Bellagio.
- Night race (img): black sky, track lit by street lamps (tall single/double-arm cobra poles ~12 m along Blvd) plus building light; hotel facades glowing gold/orange (#f2b866), neon pink/purple/cyan; LED billboards and the Sphere's changing colour exosphere; track surface looks dark grey with strong reflections; cold air (10-15 C), no humidity haze, clear.
- Surface colour (img 2025): asphalt dark grey (#3c3e42) with brownish/grey streaks; thin blue painted edge/racing line (#4a78c8) on the track edge on run-offs and pit-lane exit; kerbs tricolour (white with red, small).

## Pit building and paddock (pits left)
- Pit building ("Grand Prix Plaza", web): ~300,000 sq ft, 4 levels, 16-22 m high, NE corner of Harmon Ave and Koval Ln; garages ground level on the SE side along the pit lane; team/club suites on levels 2-3; rooftop terrace and a huge LED screen on the roof (web). Pit side is left of the start straight (per task).
- Look (img gp2025 54941208317/8502, 54942282868): dark glazed/charcoal tiered grandstand structure stepping back on top of the garages, white and dark cladding with a long white LED strip underside; garage fronts open, floor light grey concrete; overhead lighting rigs; green painted pit exit stripe; lane markers (white arrow, yellow line). A very large green gantry sign spans the start line with a LED clock (brand logo - replace with neutral).
- Pit lane (est) ~16 m wide light-grey concrete; pit wall left covered by a white barrier + low fence.

## Grandstands
- Main "Grand Prix Plaza" stand on top of the pit building, ~325 m long and ~24 m high (web), five stand structures along East Harmon (web); dark charcoal frame with banners; mid-tier suites with glass.
- Temporary stands along Harmon, Koval, Strip (web); Bellagio Fountains Club and similar hospitality on the Blvd.
- (img) the main stand looks continuous with lit sponsor boards, multi-level, with spectators visible at rails.

## Top 10 priorities
1. Night look: pitch-black sky, orange/gold lit hotel facades, pink/cyan/purple neon, long street-lamp rows.
2. Strip straight flanked by large hotel proxies (100-200 m) with emissive windows; Sphere with animated colour LED shader.
3. Pit building: 4-level dark/white stepped structure with rooftop stand, white LED underside, garage fronts; long main stand.
4. Dark grey asphalt with thin blue edge lines; red/white low kerbs; pit lane light-grey with green exit stripe.
5. Concrete barrier + 4-5 m fence, with sponsor-colour (neutral) panels green/blue/white.
6. Eiffel-like lit tower, Bellagio-like fountain with animated jets, High Roller wheel as skyline markers.
7. Harmon Corner curved LED screen at T14-T16.
8. Wide 6-8 lane boulevard, palm and cypress trees, raised pedestrian bridges with lit glass walls.
9. Desert mountains in the distance, cool clear night air (no haze).
10. Reflective surface after dark (subtle specular on asphalt), car park and low commercial blocks between towers.
