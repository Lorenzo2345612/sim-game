## Overall
- Layout (web): 4.927 km, 19 turns, anticlockwise; T1-T3 Kallang/Sheares, T4-T19 Downtown Core; since 2023 the float/Memorial section is removed and a ~400 m Raffles Avenue straight runs T14-T16. Start/finish and the pit building are on Republic Boulevard beside the Singapore Flyer.
- Relief: essentially flat; Anderson Bridge, Esplanade Bridge and the Benjamin Sheares Bridge (elevated expressway, img "pit entry" / "flyer 02") are the only vertical features.
- Environment (img): equatorial city; glass/steel skyscrapers 150-280 m (Raffles Place CBD) across the bay, colonial low-rise (Fullerton, City Hall, Padang), Esplanade "durian" domes with sun-shade fins, Singapore Flyer (165 m, wheel + base building), Marina Bay Sands (3 towers + ship deck, 200 m). Wide lawns (Padang), tropical trees (rain trees, palms), waterfront promenade.
- Night race (img): pitch-black sky; ~1,600 floodlights on ~6-9 m poles with curved arms, spaced ~20 m, white (~#fff6e0, cool-white LED) at ~3 000+ lux on track; buildings lit warm/yellow with blue/white glass towers; reflections on the water; advertising boards lit; pit building lit by strip lights along its upper edge and white rails.
- Climate: hot and humid (~30 C), hazy sky even at dusk; surfaces dark, slightly dusty.

## Pit building and paddock (pits left)
- Side: left of the pit straight as you head along Republic Boulevard (img homestraight, grid). Permanent F1 Pit Building (also used for other events), alongside the Singapore Flyer.
- Size (est): ~350 m long, 3 storeys, ~16 m high, white/light mint rendered concrete with long horizontal glazing bands and a sweeping curved roof in mint/teal green, garage doors on the ground floor (img "Pit building", "prep2023_13").
- Pit lane: concrete asphalt, pit wall fenced with a mesh/wire at the lane side; pit exit marked by a white arrow and painted line (img "pit exit").
- Paddock: behind the pit building on the Flyer side, with a temporary road from the pits under the Sheares Bridge (web).
- Garages (img): 3 m tall roll-doors, red-lined floors, grey epoxy floor; scrutineering hall at the paddock side.

## Grandstands
- Temporary scaffold stands, 12-25 rows, steel frame in grey/galvanised, seats in blue (#2f6fc2) and yellow (#f0b800) forming large patterns (img "race", "race2022"); canopy over some premium stands.
- Padang stage (img "padang": large red truss stage, LED screens, 12 m high) plus Padang grandstands; Raffles Avenue stands in front of the Esplanade; Pit Straight main stand opposite the pit building (img "waterfront": blue/yellow rows with a hotel tower behind).
- Capacity: not verified (est tens of thousands).

## Top 10 priorities
1. Night lighting: rows of white floodlights on 6-9 m curved-arm poles every ~20 m beside the track; black sky; emissive buildings.
2. Continuous 4-5 m steel mesh fence with posts and a white concrete barrier base with yellow band.
3. Pit building left: long white building with curved mint roof and horizontal glazing; ground-floor garage doors.
4. Painted kerbs: red/white with big black letters; blue + yellow and green/white painted run-offs.
5. Blue/yellow-seat scaffold stands along Republic Blvd/Raffles Ave and the Padang.
6. CBD glass skyline + Marina Bay Sands + Flyer on the background (billboards/imposters, emissive windows).
7. Anderson Bridge and Esplanade Bridge decks with white rails and strip lights; water reflections.
8. Sheares expressway piers/deck looming over T1 section.
9. Colonial low-rise (Fullerton, City Hall, Padang) in cream/white with arched windows.
10. Warm humid haze: low contrast distance fog tinted dark teal-grey at night.
