# Spielberg (Red Bull Ring) - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Alpine foothills, Murtal valley, Styria; track sits on a hillside shelf with the Styrian mountains (Seckauer Alpen) behind, pine forest and meadow farmland |
| Relief | ~65 m per lap (sources up to 84 m), climbs to T1 up to ~12 %, T2 fast downhill kink, T3 uphill, T4 steep downhill, T5 hairpin on a downhill braking zone, T6-T7 left-hander pair, T8 uphill, T9/T10 fast right-handers onto the straight; wide hillside bowl visible from the stands |
| Vegetation | Spruce/pine forest dark green #2F4A2B on slopes, bright meadows #7FA03F with wildflowers, occasional deciduous trees; no trees close to the track |
| Sky/weather | alpine blue with cumulus, frequent afternoon cloud/mist; mountains faded blue-grey #7C93AA |
| Asphalt | dark grey #424245 with newer light patches |
| Grass | bright green #6FA23A, mown short beside kerbs |
| Gravel | beige/light sand #D5C7A2, narrow strips (<2.5 m) behind exit kerbs at T4/T6/T9/T10 |
| Run-off paint | T1: red #C8312C and white painted asphalt zone with large painted lettering; green #2F8F5B strip alongside kerbs; other corners: red/white paint on asphalt run-off |
| Kerbs | red/white, red/white/green striped broad kerbs; yellow/red/white sausage curbs at some exits |
| Barriers | silver armco on short posts, curved low concrete walls with tyre wall at T1/T3; wooden-green catch fences and clear-grey debris fences; tyre walls with white wraps at T1 |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Alpine mountain backdrop and green hillside meadow bowl with pine forest.
2. Steep uphill start straight to T1 and downhill T4-T5 (65 m range).
3. T1 red/white painted run-off with big painted marks and beige gravel.
4. Red/white (and green-striped) kerbs with narrow gravel strips.
5. Grey glass pit building on the right with long paddock and motorhomes.
6. Terraced hillside grandstands with red/white spectators.
7. Rust-steel arch with bull proxy near the paddock (generic, no logos).
8. Silver armco + low curved concrete walls + tyre walls.
9. Bright cumulus alpine sky, wildflower meadows with no trees near track.
10. Short, tight 10-corner layout with long blend-uphill sections.
