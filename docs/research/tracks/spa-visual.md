# Spa - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 44 local files in `.ai/refs/spa/` (git-ignored). Unrelated files (s32, s94-s99) were deleted. All images viewed. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Ardennes forested valley, steep relief ~100 m elevation change La Source to Les Combes |
| Vegetation | Norway spruce plantations (dark green #2B4A2A) mixed with birch/beech/oak (lighter #5C8A3A, autumn yellow); clear-cuts and fields on valley sides; very dense, trees to 25 m |
| Sky/weather | frequently overcast, damp, mist in valley; sunny days with blue sky also common |
| Asphalt | dark grey #3B3B3E, light patches where resurfaced |
| Grass | bright green #6A9A3A, mown short next to track |
| Run-off paint | Raidillon/La Source: red #D6403F, yellow #F2C230, white stripes (Belgian-flag pattern) plus green #2FB59A painted asphalt |
| Kerbs | red/white (and red/yellow) flat+serrated; green strip edges |
| Barriers | armco with green or grey chain-link/mesh debris fences on steel posts; concrete walls with white paint; tyre walls at Bus Stop and La Source; red/white barrier blocks and red/white tyre walls (s18, s45) |
| Fences | tall green chain-link debris fencing on grey posts (s86, s24-s26) |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Steep Ardennes relief with Eau Rouge dip and Raidillon climb.
2. Dark spruce forest belts, mixed birch/beech, with clearings.
3. Belgian-flag pattern painted run-off at Eau Rouge/Raidillon and green painted aprons at La Source.
4. Green coated chain-link debris fences and grey/green armco.
5. Long grey 2-storey pit building with open lattice roof and red-roofed stand at the end.
6. Control tower stack with brown roof near pit entry.
7. Blue seat temporary stands on hillsides at Les Combes, Blanchimont, Bus Stop.
8. White/grey weathered concrete walls with white paint at pit wall.
9. Overcast damp sky, low mist in valley.
10. Marshal posts in yellow/orange with red/white barrier blocks at corners.
