# Mexico City (Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez) - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 77 local files in `.ai/refs/mexico/` (git-ignored, untrusted, not for `assets/`); several are history B&W (1962) and layout diagrams. Many CC0 (Gobierno CDMX) and CC BY-SA 4.0. Colours are eyeball estimates; coordinates approximate. Licenses read from Commons metadata (API). Sponsor boards must not be reproduced. Foro Sol / stadium names are real venue names (not brand marks of the series); use generic "stadium section".

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Flat urban park (Magdalena Mixhuca sports city) in east Mexico City at 2,238 m altitude (thin air); lat/lon 19.4061, -99.0925 (Wikipedia 19°24′22″N 99°5′33″W); dense housing outside, ponds/lake and trees inside the park |
| Relief | essentially flat (a few metres); slight rise on main straight; Peraltada was banked, now bypassed |
| Vegetation | mature eucalyptus/ash/pine tree belts around the park perimeter, mown lawn, flower beds; trees behind fences #4F7A3A |
| Sky/weather | clear blue (#7AA8D8) with high clouds, smog haze on horizon; mountains faint far east |
| Asphalt | dark grey #3E3E42 |
| Grass | bright green #6FA83A (heavily irrigated) |
| Run-off paint | red/white painted run-off, green #2FA060 painted edge strips beside kerbs; sections with blue-grey paint on Formula E infield |
| Kerbs | red/white + green outside strip, very wide at slow corners; rumble strips along main straight edge |
| Barriers | green/white plastic-wrapped concrete/Tecpro walls (green #3E8E4E), tall chain-link catch fences, tyre walls; light grey pit wall |

## Pit building and paddock

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right of main straight (matches PRD) |
| Size | new 2015 building ~330 m, 3 levels (~14 m): garages at ground, glazed hospitality/control above, rooftop |
| Facade | light grey/beige panels, dark glass band, yellow sign band (sponsor colour; use plain yellow/grey strip), garage doors white roller shutters |
| Roof | flat with slight overhang |
| Pit lane | dark asphalt with red/white edge and white lines; pit entry near the end of the main straight, exit before T1 |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Flat, high-altitude park with irrigated bright grass.
2. Foro Sol bowl with a track running through the infield.
3. Long truss grandstand with sloped dark canopy on main straight.
4. Green plastic-wrapped barrier walls and tall catch fences.
5. Red/white kerbs with painted green edge strips and wide red/white paint run-off.
6. Light grey 3-level pit building with a yellow sign band and glazed top.
7. Dome of Palacio de los Deportes on the skyline.
8. Dense low housing and tree belts outside the fence; haze.
9. Pond/lake inside the park.
10. Clear blue sky with smog haze.
