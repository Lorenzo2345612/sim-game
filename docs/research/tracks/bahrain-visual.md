# Bahrain (Sakhir) - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 40 local files in `.ai/refs/bahrain/` (git-ignored, untrusted). 0000 and 0148 are off-topic (navy base photos; useless, can be deleted); 0020 is a layout diagram. 38 usable. All viewed. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate. Sponsor boards, liveries and logos visible in photos must NOT be reproduced.

Layout: GP layout 5.412 km, 15 turns, clockwise, pits on the right of the main straight (north side), 26.0325 N 50.5106 E. First night race 2014; capacity ~70,000.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Flat limestone desert; golden-ochre rocky outcrops and low cliffs behind the track (#C9A26A), sparse scrub, pale sand (#E6D8B8) over infield and surroundings (SkySat 0003/0005), low hazy industry on the horizon |
| Relief | Almost flat; mild rise on T7-T8 section and crest at T12; man-made berms and rock faces (0216, 0218 top edge) |
| Vegetation | Very sparse; date palms and irrigated green lawns only around buildings and entrance; bright strips of mown grass (#5E9A3A) beside the main straight (0122) and inside T1 |
| Sky/lighting | Day: hazy pale blue-white, strong low-contrast sun, dust haze. Night: black sky, dense white-yellow floodlight wash |
| Asphalt | Mid-dark grey #55575A, slightly lighter and coarse (graywacke aggregate); newer patches near-black #3A3B3D |
| Sand/ground | Pale cream #E6D8B8; stabilised with sprayed binder |
| Run-off paint | Cream/sand painted asphalt #D8C9A0 with red (#C8352F) swoosh/stripe patterns and orange accents (0218); T1 outside dark blue #2A3C78; blue/teal/green triangulated mosaic (#1F7FA8, #2FA59A, #3B6FB5) on large run-off areas and infield (0218 top, SkySat) |
| Kerbs | Flat red #C8352F / white, low profile; some with yellow edge line; white track-limit lines |
| Gravel | Pale grey pea-gravel strips inside some corner exits (0216) |
| Barriers | Grey armco double/triple rails (0216, 0218 top); white concrete walls; red/white striped concrete blocks with orange ends at pit exit and T1 (0122); sloped grey chain-link catch fencing on grey posts in front of stands; orange/white marker bollards |
| Overhead | Yellow-board gantries (generic yellow boards, drop the text) over the track after T1 and at pit exit (0218); start gantry with light bar (0122) |

## Night lighting

Tall slender poles (~25-30 m, some ~15 m) with clusters of 6-12 white floodheads, spaced 50-80 m behind fences and stands; bright white-yellow wash on the track; stand roofs lit amber from below; palms up-lit; belvedere arches coloured blue/purple; black sky (0002, 0231).

## Top 10 priorities

1. Pale cream sand terrain with ochre rock outcrops, not grass; green only around buildings.
2. Large sand-coloured and dark-blue painted run-offs with red swoosh shapes; blue/teal mosaic infield.
3. Red/white flat kerbs, white track-limit lines.
4. Main grandstand with white tensile sail roof plus belvedere arches.
5. Sakhir tower proxy (elliptical glass stack + tent crown + flag), ~55 m.
6. Pit building: 400 m, 2 storeys, cream concrete, dark doors with red number headers.
7. Paddock: 4-5 white peaked tents, helipads.
8. Sloped chain-link catch fences, white concrete walls, red/white blocks with orange ends.
9. Floodlight poles 25-30 m with 6-12 heads every 60 m; dark sky for night variant.
10. Date palms and green lawn patches at entrance/paddock; hazy low-contrast sky.
