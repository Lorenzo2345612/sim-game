# Cleveland (Burke Lakefront Airport) - visual reference

60 local images in `.ai/refs/cleveland/` (git-ignored; never copied to assets/public). Layout images: maps only. Race photos on Commons are scarce (one 1988 photo), so surroundings are documented from airport and city photos. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate. Public domain or CC BY / CC BY-SA; keep Sources for credit. No series/sponsor marks to be reproduced.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Temporary circuit on runways/taxiways of Burke Lakefront Airport (~41.517 N, -81.683 W), narrow peninsula on the Lake Erie shore, downtown to SW |
| Layout | 1997-2007 version 2.106 mi (3.389 km), ~10 turns, flat; earlier 1982-89 version 2.480 mi |
| Relief | Dead flat, ~175 m above sea level |
| Surface | Pale grey concrete runways #B8B8B4 with white markings (threshold bars, numbers, centrelines), darker asphalt patches/taxiways #6A6A6C, visible expansion joints |
| Grass | wide mown infield, #6F9040 green; dry yellow-brown #9A8B4E in summer |
| Lake edge | grey limestone riprap/seawall #8C8C88, open water #4F7F9A, flat horizon |
| Barriers | grey concrete Jersey/temporary walls with painted ad panels, ~4 m catch fence on inward-leaning posts, tyre barriers at tight corners; no permanent run-offs; runway width gives asphalt run-off |
| Vegetation | very sparse: some shrub lines and small trees on perimeter; no forests |
| Sky | big open lake sky, often hazy, strong wind |

## Pit area

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right of start/finish (matches PRD) |
| Structures | temporary pit lane on a taxiway/runway edge: low concrete wall, temporary garage tents/trailers, paddock on apron beside airport buildings |
| Backdrop | Burke terminal (mid-century, low concrete/glass), blocky grey control tower with fence, pale barrel-roof hangar (Landmark Aviation) and darker arched hangars |

## Grandstands

| Location | Description |
|---|---|

| Corners | scaffold terraces at hairpin and T7/T8 box corners; spectator areas on grass behind fences |
| Hospitality | white tents on apron |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Pale grey concrete runway slabs with white markings and joints.
2. Wide flat grass infield, dry-yellow tint option.
3. Lake Erie riprap edge and open water on the north side.
4. Concrete Jersey walls with catch fence on leaning posts.
5. Temporary scaffold grandstands.
6. Airport terminal, tower, hangars as backdrop proxies.
7. Downtown skyline silhouette to the SW.
8. Browns stadium + Rock Hall + Science Center cluster.
9. Shoreway on south edge.
10. Almost no trees; large sky.
