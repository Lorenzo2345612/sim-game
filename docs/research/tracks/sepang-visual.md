# Sepang - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 39 local files in `.ai/refs/sepang/` (git-ignored, untrusted). Viewed: aerials, tower/grandstand, entrance, pit lane/T15 area, grids, 2016 opening lap, drain cover, 4h-race grid. The remaining driver/pit-stop shots (0038, 0105, 0126, 0137, 0985, 1105, 1121, 1134, 1142) were only skimmed by title and add little. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate. Sponsor boards, liveries and logos in photos must NOT be reproduced (use plain teal/green boards without text).

Layout: 5.543 km, 15 turns, clockwise, 16-22 m wide, pits on the right of the main straight; 2.7606 N, 101.7375 E (about 45 km S of Kuala Lumpur, next to the airport). Back straight (0.93 km) runs parallel to the pit straight; two hairpin "butterfly" ends (T1-T2 and T14-T15) mirror each other (aerial 0003).

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|

## Top 10 priorities

1. Tropical palette: bright lawn greens, dark palm/plantation backdrop, hazy white sky; cumulus clouds.
2. Painted red/white/yellow/blue run-off banks at hairpins (T15, T1) plus thin blue edge lines.
3. Main grandstand with tensile petal roof and the umbrella-canopy tower with a globe on top.
4. Pit building: grey panel cladding, green-tinted glass, timing tower with 3 columns.
5. Teal-green hoardings along pit wall and fences (no text).
6. Red/white kerbs; pea gravel at T1 exit.
7. Palm rows (royal/oil palms) along the pit straight and behind stands; hedge behind the main stand.
8. Brick-paved concourse and entrance saddle roof.
9. Hillstand bank (T9-T11) as grassy rise with temporary tiers.
10. Wet-weather variant: heavy rain, dark sky (2012 grid).
