# Road Atlanta (braselton) - visual reference

83 local images in `.ai/refs/braselton/` (git-ignored; never copied to assets/public). Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate (+-200 m). All images carry attribution (CC BY / CC BY-SA 2.0-4.0, a few CC0); keep the Sources list for credit. Sponsor banners (blue tyre-maker banners, etc.) seen in photos must NOT be reproduced.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Permanent road course in dense Georgia Piedmont forest (Braselton, Hall County, ~34.1467 N, -83.8178 W) |
| Layout | Clockwise, 12 turns, ~4.09 km (2.54 mi), ~24 m (79 ft) elevation change; rolling hills, blind crests, fast downhill T12 |
| Vegetation | Mixed pine and hardwood, 15-25 m, dark green; trees 5-20 m behind fences; exposed red clay cuts and terraced hillsides |
| Asphalt | mid/dark grey #4A4A4C, white edge lines (~15 cm), worn patches #5A5A5C |
| Grass | mown strips #6F8F3A, forest shade #4A6A2C; dry late-season #8A8A4A |
| Gravel / sand | tan-orange #C9B58A to #BFA27A beds at corner exits (see `Throwing_Gravel`, `GRT_Spin`) |
| Red clay | #B5602F on cut banks and old dirt tracks |
| Kerbs | low serrated blue / yellow / green stripes (also red-white at some corners); painted apron strips on exits |
| Walls | pale-grey/white concrete walls #C8C8C4 topped by 3-4 m chain-link catch fence on grey steel posts; tyre stacks (black/white) at a few apexes; armco on some run-offs |
| Sky | humid southeastern, hazy blue to overcast; night racing under tall floodlight poles |
| Details | white corner-worker booths with green roofs; aircraft-visibility markers overhead on back straight; overhead bridge at T11 |

## Pit area

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right of start/finish (matches PRD) |
| Length | ~300 m long pit lane/wall; new infield pit/paddock complex under the hillside |
| Structures | long white concrete pit wall with fence; low garage row with awnings on infield terrace at foot of a cut hill; paddock tents/trucks behind; start-line banner gantry/bridge |
| Colours | wall off-white #D0D0CC, garage roofs white/grey, red clay hillside behind |
| Race control | tower/building above the pit straight (see 2016 aerial) |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Dense pine/hardwood tree line 5-20 m from track, full canopy, red-clay cuts.
2. Elevation: rolling hills, crests at T2/T3, downhill T12.
3. Pale-grey concrete walls with tall chain-link catch fence.
4. Blue/yellow/green low serrated kerbs.
5. Tan-orange gravel beds at corner exits plus mown grass strips.
6. Long white pit wall + infield garage row at foot of hillside.
7. T11 overhead bridge and start-line gantry.
8. Tall floodlight poles (night race option).
9. Green-roof corner-worker booths.
10. Hillside spectator terraces with tents instead of big stands.
