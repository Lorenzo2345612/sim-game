## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Royal park (Parco di Monza), flat, dense mature deciduous forest hugging the whole track; old banked oval inside/west of the road course; golf course to the north |
| Relief | Essentially flat (a few metres at most); Lesmos and Parabolica very gently sloped |
| Sky/weather | Late-summer Italian: clear to hazy blue, strong sun, hard shadows from tree line |
| Vegetation | Oak, ash, aspen, plane; tall (20-30 m), full canopy, dark green with some yellowing; dense walls 5-15 m behind the fences; mown grass strips between asphalt and barriers |
| Asphalt | dark grey #3A3A3C, bleached to #55555A where worn; white edge lines ~15 cm |
| Grass | #6E8B3D (sunlit) to #4C6B2E (shade) |
| Gravel | light beige #CFC4A8 (outside of chicanes, Ascari, Parabolica) |
| Run-off paint | Italian tri-colour: green (#1F9A6A) apron strips along track edge (Parabolica, Rettifilo, Roggia, banking curve), red (#C8452E) and white bands in wide asphalt run-off at Rettifilo; yellow/black serrated raised cut-through strip (p100/p104/p112) |
| Kerbs | red #C8201E / white #EDEDED, low flat/serrated profile; tri-colour (red/white/green) kerb variants at Ascari exit and Rettifilo (p56, p59) |
| Barriers | galvanised double armco (grey-white), orange/red-white chevron barrier blocks (p114/p116), green-painted wall at Ascari exit (p56), concrete walls, tyre walls, concrete walls, tyre walls at chicanes, 4-5 m chain-link catch fence on steel posts (grey) |
| Banking | weathered light-grey concrete, yellow-ish centre line, armco at top, trees growing through; visible from track at Curva Grande side and Parabolica approach (`52_Monza_old_ring.jpg`) |

## Pit building and paddock

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right of the start/finish straight in running direction (matches PRD) |
| Size | ~350 m long, 3 storeys + terrace, ~15 m high; separate control tower at race-control end (~5 storeys, glazed cylinder with curved concrete roof slab) |
| Facade | ground floor: grey roller-shutter garages with blue numerals (43-48 visible); upper floors: glazed stepped curtain wall, aluminium frames, grey-blue glass; concrete/grey-blue panel base |
| Roof | cream/white tensile canopy on steel columns forming a row of low peaks over the 3rd-level terrace |
| Pit lane | width ~12-13 m; red painted fast-lane stripe plus white lines (recent), blue strip in older photos; concrete pit wall with steel railings and mesh fence; entry before Parabolica exit, exit after first chicane run-off |
| Podium | cantilevered platform above pit lane; blue/white check backdrop (no logos) |
| Paddock | white tents/trucks behind the pit building (south), visible in SkySat `06_`/`07_` |
| Gantry | green-painted steel lattice footbridge over the start straight with light poles (`29_Circuito_de_Monza10.jpg`); another yellow/red box-girder bridge after the first chicane (omit sponsor) |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Dense tall deciduous tree wall on both sides (instanced, 15-25 m) - the strongest Monza cue.
2. Long 3-storey glazed pit building with cream tent roof and grey garage shutters, pits on the right.
3. Green steel footbridge/gantry over the start straight and cantilever podium.
4. Red/white flat kerbs with painted asphalt run-off (green/red) at Rettifilo and Ascari.
5. Tyre walls with red/white barrier blocks at chicanes; catch fence on grey posts.
6. Old banking concrete wedges visible behind trees.
7. Cream mid-century start-line grandstand opposite pits.
8. Light beige gravel traps outside Ascari/Parabolica.
9. Dark asphalt with white edge lines; yellow/red start line chequered strip.
10. Orange marshal posts and low advertising boards (generic text only).
