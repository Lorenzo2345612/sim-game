# Detroit (downtown street circuit) - visual reference

60 local images in `.ai/refs/detroit/` (git-ignored; never copied to assets/public). Race images: 2024 event photos (CC BY-SA 4.0), 1980s F1 shots, maps. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate. CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / public domain; keep Sources for credit. Sponsor boards (car-maker script and logo on barriers, tower signage, "GM" logos) must NOT be reproduced. Uncertainty: pit/start-finish placement differs between sources (Wikipedia says Atwater; the 2023 map shows start on Jefferson side) - verify against repo OSM data before modelling.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Downtown city street circuit, Detroit River waterfront (~42.329 N, -83.04 W); 2023+ layout ~2.65 km (1.645 mi), 9-10 turns; repo OSM centerline 2562 m |
| Relief | Flat, small drop down Bates toward river, uphill at Rivard |
| Surface | mixed asphalt #55555A and concrete panels #A8A8A4, patched, with painted lines |
| Walls | concrete barriers #BDBDB8 topped by galvanised posts with diagonal wire-mesh fence #9A9EA0 (about 4 m); black advertising boards on barrier face; black pedestrian railing on spectator side |
| Kerbs | low red/white or neutral painted street kerbs; few, mostly at hairpin and chicanes |
| Run-off | almost none; tyre barriers at some corners |
| Vegetation | street trees, mid-green #4E7A34, small planters; no forest |
| Buildings | beige/tan stone and concrete mid-rise (#C8B99A to #D8CCB0) 60-100 m with window grids; dark-glass towers; Renaissance Center dominates the east |
| Sky | overcast/grey in 2024 photos, humid |

## Pit area

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right (matches PRD) |
| Layout | double-sided pit lane: 14 boxes on one side, 13 on the other (official), on a wide street near Franklin/St Antoine; exit left uphill to Rivard |
| Structures | temporary garages/tents with fabric walls, pit wall as concrete barrier + fence, overhead gantries |
| Colours | grey barriers, white tents, black boards |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Continuous concrete wall + galvanised post and wire-mesh fence along the whole lap.
2. Black pedestrian railing and spectator side platforms.
3. Dense beige/tan mid-rise building walls with window grids on Jefferson.
4. Renaissance Center cluster in dark bronze glass east of the circuit.
5. Huntington Place (curved glass) at the hairpin.
6. Wide temporary grandstands and pedestrian bridges.
7. Open river side along Atwater with Windsor skyline.
8. Mixed asphalt/concrete surface with patches.
9. People Mover guideway and Hart Plaza.
10. Street trees, lamps, planters; no gravel.
