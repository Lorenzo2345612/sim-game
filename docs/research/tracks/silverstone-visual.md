# Silverstone - visual reference (city/venue name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 75 local files (all viewed; s11, n118, n129, n155 deleted as unrelated) in `.ai/refs/silverstone/` (git-ignored). Mostly geograph (CC BY-SA 2.0) ground shots; aerials from SkySat and Flickr. Many show sponsor marks; omit them in the game. Colours are eyeball estimates. Coordinates approximate.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Former WWII airfield, flat Northamptonshire farmland; hedges, scattered oaks, poplar rows; conifer plantation to the north-east; open fields all around; temporary campsites and car parks |
| Relief | Almost flat (+-5 m); long straights |
| Sky/weather | Changeable grey/white cumulus, often overcast, rain possible (`s263`, `s333` dark storm clouds) |
| Asphalt | mid-dark grey #4A4A4D (lighter than Monza), very wide, grey surface with rubber lines |
| Grass | mown #6DA03A, drier straw #A59B5A in summer |
| Gravel | dark grey-brown #7A6C5A, tan at Copse/Becketts |
| Run-off | wide tarmac aprons (grey) and gravel; green painted tarmac patches near Abbey; red painted strips under barriers |
| Kerbs | red/white flat and low serrated; red/white rounded concrete "sausage" inside kerbs at Brooklands/Luffield; green-painted (#2EA06A) tarmac apron strips along edges (n175-n179) |
| Barriers | orange/red foam-block barrier walls and armco, white-painted concrete walls with advertising hoardings (omit marks), concrete walls painted white, 3-4 m chain-link catch fence on angled grey steel posts (very recognisable) |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Flat open airfield/farmland, hedges and rows of poplar/oak, wide open sky.
2. The Wing: folded grey roof planes with red edge, glazed top, anthracite garages.
3. Blue painted pit-lane working strip with white lines and galvanised stair/railings.
4. Angled-post chain-link catch fence along all grass edges.
5. Tyre walls in white with orange/red blocks at Copse, Luffield.
6. Long scaffold grandstands with white roofs and blue seats.
7. Wide tarmac aprons and tan gravel traps.
8. Green-draped footbridges and screen gantries across the straight.
9. Overcast cumulus sky; grass straw-tinted in summer.
10. Scoreboard tower (generic) at Luffield and white painted concrete wall at Woodcote.
