## Overall
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | 29.18556, -81.06944 (Daytona Beach, FL), next to Daytona Beach Intl Airport |
| Environment | Flat coastal Florida; sandy soil, palms, live oaks; ~180 acre infield with 29-acre Lake Lloyd |
| Layout | Sports-car course 3.56 mi, 12 turns; NASCAR road course 3.57 mi, 14 turns (adds bus-stop chicane). Pits on the left (tri-oval front straight) |
| Banking | Oval turns 31 deg, tri-oval/front stretch 18 deg, back straight 3 deg, infield flat. Straights: front 3800 ft, back 3000 ft |
| Elevation | Effectively flat except banking; infield tunnel under oval T1 (built 2004) |
| Sky | Bright Florida blue, cumulus, strong sun, hard shadows |

## Top 10 priorities
1. Two banked sections (31 deg) in the oval corners, flat infield, banking wall white with black block letters.
2. Tall silver chain-link catch fence on posts along the banking and front stretch.
3. Huge continuous front stand with multicolour seat patches and a long roof with light masts.
4. Left-side pit lane with garage block, wide asphalt, pit wall gantry.
5. Lake Lloyd in the infield and the tunnel under oval T1.
6. Flat, bright green grass with painted infield logos; palms outside.
7. Bus Stop chicane with gravel and tyre markers on the backstretch.
8. Painted blue/white run-off and blue-white SAFER walls in the infield.
9. Scoring pylon tower and light-mast rows.
10. Florida blue sky, hard sun; minimal terrain relief.
