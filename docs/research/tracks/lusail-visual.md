# Lusail - visual reference (city name only; no series/brand marks)

Images: 29 local files in `.ai/refs/lusail/` (git-ignored, untrusted). IMPORTANT LIMIT: Commons has NO free-licensed photos of the 2021+ Lusail F1-era buildings (Category:Losail International Circuit has 7 direct files; all other files sit in old motorcycle-GP categories, 2009-2022, taken on the OLD Losail layout/buildings; Getty-type galleries are editorial and were rejected). Usable here: night lighting, floodlight masts, old pit/stand look, fencing, run-off ground, layouts. For the 2023 buildings rely on the text facts below and flag as "unverified visually". Many files (0066, 0105, 0122, 0124, 0127, 0133, 0137, 0138, 0143, 0171, 0172, 0177, 0198, 0235) are rider portraits/group photos with little visual value; kept only for stand/lighting/pit-edge backgrounds. Colours are eyeball estimates; coordinates approximate. Sponsor boards and liveries in photos must NOT be reproduced.

Layout: 5.419 km, 16 turns (10 right, 6 left), clockwise, main straight 1.068 km, 25.4900 N 51.4542 E (Wikipedia 25 29 24 N 51 27 15 E), Tilke design, opened 2004 (Losail 5.380 km), rebuilt 2021-23, capacity ~52,000 (was 8,000); full permanent floodlighting (night racing); pits right of the main straight; layout diagrams 9000 (F1 2023) and 9001.

## Overall

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Environment | Flat desert north of Doha, ~10 m above sea level; no vegetation beyond the track, surrounding ground beige sand #CDB892 and compacted gravel; the 2023 rebuild adds landscaped paddock/VVIP gardens with native plants and 2,577 trees (text source); circuit ringed by artificial grass to hold sand |
| Relief | Dead flat; low sand berms; tunnels under the track (3 new in rebuild) |
| Sky/lighting | Race at night under permanent floodlights: black sky, white-yellow light masts, grey-yellow haze; daytime hazy pale blue-white (0177 background) |
| Asphalt | Dark grey-green #4A4C4E at night, coarse; resurfaced 2021-23 |
| Grass | Artificial turf strips beside the track #4F8A3A -> bright green in light, thin strips ~2-5 m wide (0004, 0002, 0019) |
| Run-off | Beige sand/gravel #C9B38A in banks (0004 foreground); paved cream run-off areas outside some corners; per-turn paint (see table): orange, blue, maroon |
| Kerbs | Red/white flat kerbs (0004 bottom); 2023 kerbs were pyramid style, reshaped in 2024 (rounded tops at T1, 2, 4, 10, 12, 13, 14) |
| Barriers | Tall teal-green mesh fences #2F7F6A on green posts with sloped tops (0002, 0003, 0000) -- the signature look; white concrete walls with dark/black advertising panels; lower white wall with grey top; yellow overhead gantry (generic) over the straight (0004) |
| Pit-lane edge | Purple/maroon painted band along the pit-lane edge (0235, 0122) and maroon-white line at the pit wall (0019) |

## Pit building and paddock

Old (2004-2020, in photos): long, low, 2-storey beige/grey building about 250 m; ground-floor garage doors terracotta-pink #B5645A (0000); upper storey glass-fronted lounges with white balcony rail; stepped blocks and three taller grey towers at the ends (0000, 0004); white tent-peak marquees behind (0003); start/finish overhead bridge in white sail-like shell with big arabic/latin lettering (0003); pit wall low white with a green lower band; timing tower at the end.

New (2021-23; text only, no free photos): "unverified visually"

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Side | Right of the main straight, opposite the main grandstands |
| Size | Pit building ~402 m long, 50 garages on the ground floor, paddock club on upper floor with terraces at the north end (spiral staircase, giant screen on underside), race control at the south end |
| Colours/materials | Stepped upper volumes with white ceiling edges giving horizontal banding; light-colour cladding; metal shading canopies like tree branches in the paddock |
| Team hospitality | 16 curved villas in groups of 4 behind the pit building, LED screens on first floors |
| Others | Media centre (400 people), VVIP building at the apex of T1 linked by a garden, medical centre |
| Paddock | Landscaped, native plants, ~180,000 m2 landscaping on site |

## Night lighting (verified in photos 0002, 0004, 0019, 0000, 0003)

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Masts | Slender grey poles ~25-30 m, in rows behind the barriers, roughly every 40-60 m (0002 shows ~8 in a row); heads clusters of 4-8 floodlights in rectangular panels, also 2-lamp heads on shorter poles (~15 m) and extra ring clusters mid-height (0002) |
| Light colour | Cool white #FFF8E8 with slight yellow; soft light haze/glare, no deep shadows; track evenly lit, backgrounds dark |

## Top 10 priorities

1. Night race lighting rig: rows of 25-30 m masts with 4-8 head clusters, cool white, dark sky.
2. Teal-green mesh catch fencing on green posts around the track.
3. Flat desert surroundings in beige sand with artificial-turf strips beside the track.
4. Red/white flat kerbs, painted inside areas (orange T1/T4, blue T10/T12, maroon T16).
5. 402 m, 2-storey pit building with stepped upper volumes and white ceiling bands (unverified).
6. Long single-roof steel main grandstand on the straight with underside lights.
7. White concrete walls with dark panel boards.
8. Paddock villas (16 curved) and white tents; sparse planting.
9. Beige gravel strips on exits (extra 2 m strips from 2024).
10. Layout geometry from the 2023 diagram (9000): T1 at the west end, long straight to the east.
