## Overall
- Layout (web): 6.003 km, 20 turns, anticlockwise, ~2.2 km main straight along Neftchilar Ave (seafront), walled medieval Old City (Icheri Sheher) section, narrowest 7.6 m at T8-T9. Pits/start-finish on the boulevard straight beside the bay; Government House block at the east end, Azadliq Square.
- Relief (web): gently climbing from the sea to the Old City (T8-T9 uphill), otherwise flat. Seafront boulevard is the lowest part.
- Environment (img): dense old-city blocks, 3-6 storey Neo-classical/oil-boom limestone buildings with balconies, ornate cornices, arched windows, copper-green (verdigris) domes; Soviet-era mid-rise blocks inland; modern glass towers (Flame Towers, Hilton ~25 floors) rise behind. Wide boulevard with mature pines/cypress and lawn, white/grey granite paving, tall curved lamp posts (img boulevard files).
- Sky/lighting (img): daytime race (June / since 2026 September); hazy pale-blue sky, warm low sun, strong long shadows, dry arid colour, little vegetation except boulevard park.

## Pit building and paddock (pits left)
- Side: left of the main straight (start-finish on Neftchilar/Azadliq Sq. section), a temporary building, not a permanent one (img: 2026 "image_3").
- Size (est): ~300 m long, 2 storeys (garages + hospitality/team rooms upstairs), ~9 m high, flat roof with a pitched awning above the garage doors, white/light-grey panelled temporary structure with a dark band along the garage fronts, big Qatar-style overhead banner gantries (no brands).
- Pit wall: concrete block wall + steel fence, pit-lane line white/yellow painted; lane width ~12 m (est).
- Paddock: behind the pit building in the blocks near Azadliq Sq / Government House (web); not seen in images.
- Start gantry (img Start_backshot_2016): steel truss portal over the grid with 2x5 light panels, green banner; grid slots white painted boxes, dark-green cladded barriers both sides, tall mesh above.

## Grandstands
- Temporary tubular scaffolding stands, multi-tier, across from the pits and along the Boulevard section, with spectators visible (img 2026_Grand_Prix_4: tiered, ~10-15 rows, light-grey frame, mixed coloured seats); canopy roofs on main stand (yellow/red awning visible at the pit exit in 2026_3).
- Capacity ~18,500 (web). Government House stand: lower-rise hospitality/stand under tree line (img govhouse files: yellow-orange canopy under trees, white tent).
- No large permanent stands.

## Top 10 priorities
1. Cream/sand limestone facade textures for all walls beside the track (#cdb89a) with arched windows, balconies, cornices; 3-6 storey blocks.
2. Narrow Old City: ochre stone walls (#b79d70) and crenellated towers at T8-T9; Maiden Tower proxy at the fence.
3. Concrete barrier + 4-5 m galvanised mesh fence on posts, continuous, with strip lights at the base (night-less day race).
4. Bright green (#58d23a) and red (#d0432d) painted run-offs as seen in the 2018 stills; white edge lines.
5. Government House block with its three spired towers at the east end (landmark proxy).
6. Wide seafront boulevard on the straight: lawn, pines, curved lamp posts, bay and the shoreline.
7. Pit building left (~300 m, 2 floors, white panels, dark garage fronts) and start-light gantry; temporary scaffold stands.
8. Verdigris-green domes on corner stone buildings; copper accents.
9. Flame Towers and glass towers on the skyline (blue-green glass), hazy blue sky, warm low sun with long shadows.
10. Dark slightly brown asphalt, low red/white kerbs, painted words on run-offs (use neutral text).
