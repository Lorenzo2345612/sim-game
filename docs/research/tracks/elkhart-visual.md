## Overall
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | 43.79750, -87.99389 (Plymouth/Elkhart Lake, WI), WI-67, ~640 acres |
| Environment | Northern Kettle Moraine: rolling glacial hills, mixed hardwood forest (oak, maple, birch), meadows, farmland beyond; autumn colours in fall |
| Layout | 4.048 mi (6.515 km), 14 turns, clockwise; pits on the right of the front straight |
| Relief | Track drops ~160 ft (49 m) from S/F to the straight between T11 and T12. Uphill T14-T1 (crest before T1 braking), downhill T1-T3 and into T5, uphill T5-T6, downhill T6-T7-T8, flat/downhill Carousel to Kink, flat Kink to T12, uphill T12-T13, flat T13-T14 |
| Banking | Mostly cambered; T12 apex/exit banked and uphill; Carousel is a long banked-ish constant-radius right (T9-T10, large 525 ft inside radius) |
| Sky | Midwest summer blue with scattered cumulus; fall foliage backdrop |

## Top 10 priorities
1. Rolling hills: 160 ft drop and the uphill T14-T1 crest and Canada-to-T13 hill; terrain matters more than props.
2. Dense hardwood forest hugging the Carousel, Kink and Kettle Bottoms; meadows elsewhere.
3. Very bright green grass, light grey/white concrete walls with sponsor boards (generic).
4. Red/yellow alternating kerbs at T5, T7, T8 and others; unpainted concrete rumble strips at Kink and T13.
5. Tyre walls and catch fences at T5/T8/T13.
6. Four bridges over the track (front straight, T3-5, T6, T8-9, T11) as gantry proxies.
7. Concrete walls and armco lining Kettle Bottoms.
8. Pea gravel run-offs at Carousel and T5/T6.
9. Pit lane on the right with low garages and hill paddock above; paddock tunnel.
10. Spectator hills with tents and parked cars, no big grandstands.
