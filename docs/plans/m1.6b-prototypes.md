# M1.6b — Prototypes: LMP2 and Hypercar (2026-10-13)

Models (user-downloaded from Sketchfab, CC-BY 4.0, in `assets/vendor/cars/`): `katattak-ka01` (lmp2-a, brandless original design), `ligier-js-px` (lmp2-b), `cadillac-v-series-r` (hyper-a), `ferrari-499p` (hyper-b). Class data: `docs/research/car-models.md` "Prototypes". Dynamics are calibrated by the planner side in `ai/lab/lmp2-carspec.json` and `ai/lab/hypercar-carspec.json` (lab `vehicle-model.mjs` passes with their `lab.targets`); workers copy them like GT3 (task 072). Ids/names are brand-neutral: "LMP2 A/B", "Hypercar A/B".

Inspection (2026-10-13, `.ai/scratch/inspectGlb.mjs`):
- katattak: 233k tris, length on z 4.735 m; tyre material `material_5_customtiregfgoodtire_inst_9bb5d` (57k tris); many materials; needs decimation to 70k.
- ligier: 137k tris, ONE material, 6 mesh nodes (Object_2..7): wheels must be isolated by node or by quadrant geometry.
- cadillac: 77k tris, exported ×~4 (20.6 m long; targetLength rescales); materials `Tier1Mtl` (tyre), `Rim1Mtl`, `Disc1Mtl`, `Caliper1Mtl`.
- ferrari: 32k tris; no obvious wheel material (wheels probably inside `Vehicle_Exterior_mm_misc`): wheels by quadrant geometry.
