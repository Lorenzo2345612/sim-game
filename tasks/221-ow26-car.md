## Goal

A new selectable car `ow26` ("OW-26 Hybrid", open-wheel) with ERS and active aero, calibrated to the thresholds below.

## Context

Research: `docs/research/f1-2026-car.md`. No "F1"/"Formula 1"/team names in game data or UI (PRD). SI units. Hybrid/activeAero blocks: parsed by 215, simulated by 219. Shape reference: `public/cars/f4.json`; harness reference: `tests/unit/calibration/f4.test.ts` (flat ground, closed-loop steer for lateral g). Tuning rule as task 066: once the test exists the coder may only change values in `ow26.json`; thresholds are law; report BLOCKED with evidence if impossible. Tune first with `.claude/skills/physics-lab` / `ai/lab`.

## Steps

Starting values (tune within reason): mass 768 kg incl. driver, cgHeight 0.28, wheelbase 3.4 m, track ~1.6 m, wheel radius 0.36, RWD with LSD; ICE peak ~330 N·m at 11500 rpm (~400 kW), idle 4000, limiter 15000, gearbox upRpm 14200, downRpm 9500, 8 gears; fuel capacity 100 L, startL 60, bsfc 175; carbon brakes (strong); aero cda 1.05, claFront 1.8, claRear 2.4 (Z mode); `hybrid` block = shared constants of 215 (mgukW 350000, batteryJ 4e6, deployJPerLap 8.5e6, harvestJPerLap 8.5e6, overtakeExtraJ 5e5, minSpeedMs 50/3.6, taperStartMs 290/3.6, taperEndMs 345/3.6, overtakeTaperStartMs 337/3.6, overtakeTaperEndMs 355/3.6, regenW 350000, superClipW 350000, liftHarvestW 100000); `activeAero` {dragScale 0.7, downforceScale 0.6, transitionS 0.4}; sound cylinders 6; `model.url` `/models/f4.glb` (placeholder until 224). Setup ranges and presets in the same shape as f4.json.

Conventions: speeds in km/h only in test thresholds; "0→100" measured on the first step reaching the speed; vmax = mean speed over the last 2 s; bounds inclusive.
